Mahjong Lotto — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") publishes the mobile app Mahjong Lotto (Android package name and iOS bundle ID: com.cdplus.mahjonglotto.app, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, who holds it, and the choices you have.

This document is not legal advice. It describes the App as implemented in code.

1. Information We Collect

You can play without creating an account or signing in with your name. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, postal address, precise GPS location, contacts, or photos as part of normal play.

Held only on your device (not collected by CDPLUS): lottery draw history, ticket settings, ad-frequency counters, language and appearance choices. These stay in on-device storage (SharedPreferences / local files). We do not upload them to CDPLUS servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data removes them from that device. Progress cannot be restored on another device unless optional cloud sync is available and already enabled for that install.

Processed by Google (Google AdMob) — not stored on CDPLUS servers:

Category Data Notes
Device identifiers App Set ID (Android) and similar SDK identifiers Automatic with ad requests
Advertising identifiers Android Advertising ID; iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking in ATT Optional on iOS
Usage / ad signals Ad impressions, clicks, and related measurement signals Automatic with AdMob
Network data IP address (Google may use it to infer an approximate region for ads and fraud prevention) Automatic with ad requests

Processed by Google (Firebase) when the App is configured with Firebase — not stored on CDPLUS servers:

Category Data Notes
Anonymous account Firebase Authentication anonymous user ID Created only when Firebase initializes successfully
Synced draws Draw seed and numbers stored under users/{uid}/draws/{seed} Optional cloud backup of draws

The App’s source includes Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics packages, but the App does not call Analytics events or Crashlytics APIs. Payment card details are not collected; the App has no in-app purchases. There is no separate EEA/UK/Switzerland in-app consent dialog beyond platform controls.

2. How We Use Information

Purpose What is used
Providing the game on your device Device-only progress (not collected by us)
Showing interstitial ads Advertising and device identifiers, usage signals, IP (via AdMob)
Optional cloud sync of draws Anonymous Firebase UID and draw records (via Firestore)
Answering support email you send us Whatever you include in that email

We do not send marketing newsletters. We do not operate a purchase-verification server of our own.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell personal information for money.

Advertising identifiers and related signals may be shared with Google AdMob and advertising partners that serve ads through AdMob when personalized ads are allowed (for example, after you allow tracking on iOS, or while an Android Advertising ID is available). Under some privacy laws this can count as “sharing” for cross-context advertising.

We may disclose information if required by law.

6. International Transfers

Google operates infrastructure outside Korea. Device identifiers, ad-related data, and (when Firebase is configured) anonymous account and draw records may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google runs data centers. Game progress kept only on your device is not transferred by us. Support email may be stored in the mailbox that receives it.

7. Deletion

  • Device data: uninstall the App or clear app data in system settings. We do not keep a server copy of device-only settings.
  • Firebase draws (when sync is active): removing the App does not by itself erase Google-held documents; there is no in-App delete-all-cloud button. Contact support if you need help with a synced anonymous account.
  • Advertising records: held by Google under their retention cycles. CDPLUS does not have a process to locate and delete those records for a specific user from an email alone.
  • Support email: you may ask us to delete messages we hold at the contact address below.

8. Ads and Tracking Choices

The App is free and may show interstitial ads after a set number of completed draws.

iOS App Tracking Transparency: the App requests tracking permission when appropriate. If you decline, IDFA is not used for tracking. Change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

No separate in-app consent dialog for the EEA/UK/Switzerland is implemented beyond platform controls.

Android Advertising ID: reset or delete it in device settings (path varies by OEM).

Turning off advertising identifiers may still allow non-personalized ads.

9. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing for information we hold. If we hold none beyond optional support email, we will say so. GDPR may also give you portability and objection rights.

  • Device data: delete via uninstall / clear app data.
  • Ads: use device tracking and advertising ID controls. We cannot promise deletion of Google-held records by email.
  • Support email: contact the address below. Where GDPR applies we aim to reply within one month.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not keep a user-level sale/share roster.

10. Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Support email: legitimate interest in answering you; legal obligation only where a complaint record must be kept.
  • Device identifiers, ad-related data, IP processed via Google: legitimate interests in operating a free ad-supported App. CDPLUS does not keep these records per user on its own servers.
  • Optional Firebase Auth / Firestore: legitimate interests in syncing draws when Firebase is configured.
  • iOS IDFA: consent via ATT when you allow tracking.
  • Android Advertising ID: used by AdMob while available on the device; no separate in-app consent dialog.
  • We do not rely on “contract performance” for processing personal data to run the game, because game progress on device is not collected by us as a personal profile.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not ask for age and do not mark ad requests as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. A parent may ask us to delete support email we hold.

12. Automatic Collection

The App does not use web cookies. It uses the SDKs and identifiers described above.

13. Security

  • Network traffic uses TLS.
  • Game progress stays on the device unless optional Firebase sync is active.
  • We do not store card numbers.
  • We do not operate a user personal-data database of our own. The AdMob and Firebase consoles are accessed with Google accounts.
