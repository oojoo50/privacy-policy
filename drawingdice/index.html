Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

DrawingDice ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using DrawingDice, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

(1) Directly Collected Personal Information: None

DrawingDice does not require user registration, account creation, or login.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or government-issued IDs.

(2) Automatically Collected Information via Third-Party SDKs

To deliver reliable gameplay, maintain app stability, and serve advertisements that keep the App free to enjoy, DrawingDice integrates trusted third-party software development kits (SDKs). These services may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable technical information:

  • Device & Network Information: Device model, operating system and version, screen resolution, system language, and coarse geographic location derived from IP address (country or city level; precise GPS location is never accessed).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS).
  • Usage & Diagnostic Metrics: App launch timestamps, screen navigation, feature usage statistics (such as aggregated dice roll counts), crash reports, and system performance logs.
  • In-App Purchases: Transactions for the optional 'Remove Ads' feature are processed securely by Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. We never receive or store your credit card details or sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use the Collected Information

The technical and diagnostic data collected is used solely for the following purposes:

1. Advertising Delivery: Serving personalized or contextual advertisements via Google AdMob to support ongoing development and maintenance.

2. App Stability & Bug Fixing: Detecting, diagnosing, and resolving application crashes and performance bottlenecks via Firebase Crashlytics.

3. Product Optimization: Analyzing anonymized usage patterns via Firebase Analytics to guide feature enhancements and user experience improvements.

4. Entitlement Management: Verifying and restoring the 'Remove Ads' premium entitlement across app restarts and device upgrades.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

DrawingDice relies on industry-standard services provided by Google LLC. These third parties process technical data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Services Privacy

4. Advertising Choices and Opt-Out Controls

You have complete control over how advertising identifiers are used on your device:

On Android:

  • Open Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.
  • Tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID" to opt out of interest-based ads.

On iOS:

  • Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or turn off tracking permissions specifically for DrawingDice.

💡 Completely Ad-Free Experience:

If you prefer not to see ads at all, you can make a one-time purchase of "Remove Ads" within the App. This permanently eliminates all interstitial and banner advertisements and disables ad-tracking SDK calls.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

DrawingDice does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to remove such data.

6. Data Security and Local Storage

  • All data transmitted between the App and third-party services is protected using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) encryption.
  • Your local gameplay settings, preferences, and roll history are stored exclusively on your physical device (via SharedPreferences) and are never uploaded to any remote server.

7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under data protection laws (such as GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California) regarding the processing of your data, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data processed by third-party SDK partners. You can exercise these rights directly through device privacy settings or by contacting the third-party providers listed above.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal obligations. Any updates will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
