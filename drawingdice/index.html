Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

DrawingDice ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by CDPlus, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the DrawingDice mobile application, how it is handled by third-party SDK providers, and how you can exercise your privacy choices and rights.

1. Information We Collect Directly

DrawingDice does not require account registration, sign-in, or user profiles. We do not operate our own user database or backend server, and we do not collect or store personal identifiers such as your name, address, phone number, or contacts through the App.

  • Support Inquiries: The only personal information we may hold directly is the email address and message content you voluntarily send when contacting us by email (drawingdice@googlegroups.com).
  • On-Device Gameplay & Settings Data: Your dice count, tabletop rule mode, skin preferences, language settings, and roll history are stored exclusively on your device (using local SharedPreferences). This data is never uploaded to our servers and cannot be transferred to another device. Uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your device settings permanently deletes this local data.
  • No In-App Purchases or Payment Data: DrawingDice does not offer in-app purchases or collect any billing or payment card information.
  • No Age Verification Prompt: The App does not ask for your age or date of birth.

2. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

DrawingDice integrates Google LLC services (Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config) to serve interstitial advertisements, measure anonymous app usage, diagnose crashes, and load remote configuration values. When the App communicates with these SDKs over encrypted connections, the following data may be collected and stored by Google LLC on its servers (not on CDPlus servers):

  • Advertising Identifiers:
  • iOS (IDFA): Requested via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt (requestTrackingAuthorization). If you do not allow tracking, IDFA is not accessed.
  • Android (Google Advertising ID): Accessed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK unless you delete or reset your Advertising ID in Android system settings.
  • Device & Technical Identifiers: Firebase Installation ID, app instance ID, device model, operating system version, screen dimensions, language setting, and app version.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic region (country/city level) inferred by Google from your IP address during ad and analytics requests. Precise GPS location is never requested or collected.
  • App Usage & Interaction Events: Automatic Firebase events (session start, screen views, first open) and anonymous gameplay events logged by the App (app_open, roll_completed with dice count and total, skin_changed, dice_count_changed, interstitial_shown, and interstitial_failed).
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Crash stack traces, exception reports, and runtime diagnostics collected by Firebase Crashlytics to fix bugs and improve stability.

We cannot identify a specific individual or search, view, modify, or delete individual user records in Google's systems using an email address, device model, app version, or usage timestamp.

3. Purposes of Processing and GDPR Legal Bases

Information processed in connection with DrawingDice is used only for the following purposes and legal bases (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies):

1. Serving Advertisements (Google AdMob):

  • Purpose: Displaying interstitial advertisements after dice rolls to keep the App free, measuring ad impressions, and preventing invalid traffic or ad fraud.
  • Legal Basis:
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland: Your consent collected through the Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form for personalized ads and device storage access. If you do not consent, Google serves only non-personalized or limited ads.
  • On iOS: Your consent granted via the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt for access to the IDFA.
  • On Android outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland: Legitimate interests in funding the free App via advertising while the device Advertising ID is enabled (which you may reset or delete at any time in device settings).

2. App Analytics, Remote Configuration, and Crash Diagnostics (Firebase):

  • Purpose: Understanding aggregate feature usage, tuning roll-to-ad cadence parameters (Firebase Remote Config), and fixing crashes (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Legal Basis: Legitimate interests in maintaining stability and operating the free App. Records are held by Google LLC, not by CDPlus on a per-user basis.

3. Responding to Support Emails:

  • Purpose: Answering questions or resolving complaints sent to our contact email.
  • Legal Basis: Legitimate interests in replying to your inquiry, and compliance with applicable statutory retention obligations for consumer dispute records where required by law.

4. Third-Party Recipients and International Data Transfers

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not maintain any per-user list of data sales or sharing. Technical and advertising data collected by third-party SDKs is transmitted directly to Google LLC (which may process data on servers in the United States and other countries):

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy | How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Firebase Privacy and Security

Interest-based advertising data is shared with Google's ad partners only when tracking is authorized on iOS (ATT) or the Android Advertising ID is active, and—for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland—only when consent is granted in the UMP consent form. Local gameplay history and settings remain on your device and are never transferred internationally. Support emails you send are stored in our email inbox hosted by Google.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • CDPlus (Support Emails): If you send us an inquiry by email, we retain it for up to 1 year after resolving your request, or up to 3 years if required by applicable consumer protection laws for dispute resolution, after which it is permanently deleted from our inbox.
  • Google LLC (Firebase & AdMob): Analytics and diagnostic data are not stored on CDPlus servers; they are retained and deleted by Google according to Google's retention schedules (up to 14 months for Firebase Analytics user-level data).
  • On-Device Data: Stored on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

6. Your Advertising & Tracking Choices

You can limit or withdraw consent for advertising identifiers and personalized ads at any time using the controls built into the App and your device:

  • In-App Privacy Options (UMP Consent — EEA, UK, Switzerland): On first launch in applicable regions, a consent form is presented via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When privacy options are required in your region, you can open Customise (Settings) inside the App and tap "Privacy Options" at the bottom of the sheet to review or change your consent choices at any time.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): When prompted, you can choose "Ask App Not to Track". You can also change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android Advertising ID: Open Android Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads and select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".

Note: Turning off advertising identifiers or declining personalized ads stops interest-based tracking, but non-personalized contextual ads may still be displayed during gameplay.

7. Your Privacy Rights (PIPA, GDPR, CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to processing, or data portability. Exercising your privacy rights will never restrict your access to the App.

  • On-Device Data: Delete directly by uninstalling the App or clearing App storage in your device settings. We do not hold a copy.
  • Analytics & Advertising Records: Restrict collection and use via the device and in-app settings described in Section 6. Because CDPlus does not store these records on its own servers and cannot look up individual device records in Google's systems, we do not have a procedure to locate and delete individual Google analytics or ad logs upon email request.
  • Support Emails: You (or your authorized representative with proof of authorization) may request access to or deletion of any support email you sent us by writing to the same address (drawingdice@googlegroups.com). Where the GDPR applies, we will respond within 1 month.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. You may opt out of cross-context behavioral advertising using your device's tracking/advertising ID settings above.

8. Children's Privacy

DrawingDice is a general-audience tabletop utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). The App does not include an age gate, does not distinguish between children and other users, and does not configure child-directed ad treatment (tagForChildDirectedTreatment). If a parent or legal guardian contacts us regarding a support email sent by a child, we will promptly delete that email from our inbox.

9. Security Measures

  • All network communication between the App and Google SDK endpoints uses industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Gameplay history and preferences stay exclusively in local device storage (SharedPreferences).
  • We do not operate a user database server or store payment card numbers. Access to our Firebase and AdMob consoles is restricted to authorized Google accounts.
