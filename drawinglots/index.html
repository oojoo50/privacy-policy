Royal Jebi — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") publishes the mobile app Royal Jebi (Android package name and iOS bundle ID: com.cdplus.drawinglots.app, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, who holds it, and the choices you have.

This document is not legal advice. It describes the App as implemented in code.

1. Information We Collect

You can play without creating an account or signing in. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, postal address, precise GPS location, contacts, or photos as part of normal play.

Held only on your device (not collected by CDPLUS): the language you choose in the App. It stays in on-device storage (SharedPreferences). We do not upload it to our servers. Draw progress exists only in memory while a draw is open; leaving the draw or closing the App discards unfinished numbers. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data removes the stored language from that device. Progress cannot be restored on another device.

Processed by Google (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) — not stored on CDPLUS servers:

Category Data Notes
Device identifiers Firebase installation / instance ID and similar SDK identifiers Automatic with Analytics
Usage App opens, sessions, country/language, app version, and custom events draw_start, set_complete, and share Analytics automatic collection plus events the App logs
Diagnostics Crash and error logs Crashlytics
Network data IP address (Google may use it to infer an approximate region) Automatic with Analytics

The App does not include Google AdMob, in-app purchases, or a CDPLUS backend. Payment card details are not collected. There is no separate EEA/UK/Switzerland in-app consent dialog. The App does not call App Tracking Transparency and does not collect IDFA.

If you use the system share sheet, the numbers you choose to send go to the app you pick. We do not receive a copy of that share.

2. How We Use Information

Purpose What is used
Providing the draw on your device Device-only language choice and in-memory draw state (not collected by us)
Understanding use of the App Device identifiers, usage events, approximate region via IP (via Firebase Analytics)
Stability Crash logs (via Crashlytics)
Answering support email you send us Whatever you include in that email

We do not send marketing newsletters. We do not operate a purchase-verification server. We do not log purchase statistics. AdMob is not used, so advertising is not a processing purpose.

3. How Long Information Is Kept

Data Retention

In-memory draw progress Discarded when you leave the draw or close the App
Analytics records On Google Firebase, up to 14 months from collection. Not stored on CDPLUS servers
Crash logs Per Google Crashlytics retention. Not stored on CDPLUS servers
Support emails (if you write to us) Up to 1 year after resolution; up to 3 years if the message is a complaint or dispute record required by Korean law

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell personal information for money.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics send the data in section 1 to Google so those products can run. We do not send advertising identifiers to ad networks. The App has no ads.

We may disclose information if required by law.

6. International Transfers

Google operates infrastructure outside Korea. Device identifiers, usage records, crash logs, and IP addresses may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google runs data centers. Draw progress on your device is not transferred by us. Support email may be stored in the mailbox that receives it.

7. Deletion

  • Device data: uninstall the App or clear app data in system settings. We do not keep a server copy.
  • Analytics and crash records: held by Google under their retention cycles. CDPLUS does not have a process to locate and delete those records for a specific user from an email alone.
  • Support email: you may ask us to delete messages we hold at the contact address below.

8. Ads and Tracking Choices

The App does not show ads and does not request tracking permission on iOS.

No separate in-app consent dialog for the EEA/UK/Switzerland is implemented.

Android Advertising ID: the App does not use it for ads. You may still reset or delete it in device settings.

9. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing for information we hold. If we hold none beyond optional support email, we will say so. GDPR may also give you portability and objection rights. Exercising these rights does not limit your use of the App.

  • Device data: delete via uninstall / clear app data. We have no copy.
  • Analytics and crash records: use device settings where available. We cannot promise deletion of Google-held records by email, and we do not commit to deleting those records within a set number of business days.
  • Support email: contact the address below. Where GDPR applies we aim to reply within one month. An agent may need to show authority.
  • Payment records: the App has no in-app purchases. Store records, if any, follow Apple or Google procedures.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not keep a user-level sale/share roster. If California law applies, opting out of cross-context advertising is done in device settings; this App does not run personalized ads.

10. Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Support email: legitimate interest in answering you; legal obligation only where a complaint record must be kept.
  • Device identifiers, usage records, IP processed via Google: legitimate interests in seeing how a free App is used. Google holds these records; CDPLUS does not keep them per user on its own servers.
  • We do not rely on consent for iOS IDFA; the App does not request tracking and does not collect IDFA.
  • We do not rely on “contract performance” for processing personal data to run the draw, because draw progress on the device is not collected by us as a personal profile.
  • We do not rely on a legal duty to keep transaction records; we do not hold receipts.
  • We do not process personal data for fraud checks on payments; there is no payment-verification server.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not ask for age and do not mark traffic as child-directed. We do not distinguish child users from other users. A parent may ask us to delete support email we hold. We cannot delete Google Analytics or Crashlytics records for a specific child's device from an email alone.

12. Automatic Collection

The App does not use web cookies. It uses the SDKs and identifiers described above.

13. Security

  • Network traffic uses TLS.
  • Draw progress stays in memory on the device. Language choice stays on the device. There is no cloud backup.
  • We do not store card numbers.
  • We do not operate a user personal-data database of our own. Firebase consoles are accessed with a Google account.
