BlockFit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") publishes the mobile game BlockFit (Android package name and iOS bundle ID: com.cdplus.blockfit.app, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, who holds it, and the choices you have.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the developer policies of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

You can start playing without creating an account or signing in. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, postal address, precise GPS location, contacts or photos. Game progress (cleared levels, scores, language preference and related settings) stays on your device via local storage. We do not send it to our servers or store it in the cloud.

We do not keep a file of personal information that we can look up by name or email. The only information we hold ourselves is an email you choose to send us. Advertising-related data below is processed by Google AdMob and is not stored on our servers. This App does not include the Firebase Analytics SDK and does not log custom analytics events.

Category Data How it is collected Required?
Advertising identifiers Android Advertising ID (AAID). iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the system prompt Collected automatically by the ads SDK Optional (you can turn this off)
Ad request data Device and app signals needed to serve ads, including approximate region inferred from IP Sent as part of AdMob ad requests While ads are requested
Support email Your email address and the message Only if you email oojoo50@gmail.com Optional

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising identifiers and related signals may be passed to Google AdMob and advertising partners that serve ads through it when:

  • You allow tracking on iOS, or the Android Advertising ID is turned on; and
  • In the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you consent through the UMP consent form shown by the App.

Where the CCPA applies to us, this can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can limit it as described in Section 8. We do not keep a per-person sale or share list. We may also disclose information when required by law.

6. International Transfers

Google operates servers outside the Republic of Korea. Advertising identifiers, ad request data and IP address may be transferred to the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers while you use the App. Game progress is not transferred outside your device. If you email us, that message may be stored in our mailbox at Google.

Transfers from the EU, UK and Switzerland are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. How Data Is Deleted

  • The information we hold is support email. When its retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled, we delete it.
  • Analytics and advertising records are not on our servers. Google deletes them on its own schedule.

8. Ads, Tracking and Your Choices

The App is free to play and may show banner and interstitial ads.

UMP consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • On first launch where required, the App shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent form before ads are requested.
  • Where privacy options are required, you can open Ad privacy options from the App menu and change your choices.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

  • Before accessing your IDFA, the App asks for permission through the system prompt. If you do not allow tracking, we do not collect the IDFA and ads are shown without that identifier.
  • You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android Advertising ID

  • On your device, go to Settings > Google > Ads to delete or reset your Advertising ID (menu names may vary). Once deleted, it can no longer be used for personalized ads.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

9. Your Rights and How to Use Them

We do not create user accounts. We have no file of personal information we can look up by name or email. Game progress stays on your device. Advertising data is held by Google. An email that gives only your address, device type, app version or when you played is not enough for us to find or delete that Google record.

Under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act you may ask to access, correct, delete or suspend processing of personal information we hold. If the GDPR applies to you, you may also ask us to restrict or stop processing and to receive a copy. If we do not hold the information, we will tell you that and close the request. We do not restrict your use of the App because you made a request.

On the device

Uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings. Progress on that device is removed. We have no copy.

Limiting ads

Use the UMP options where shown, turn off iOS tracking, or delete or reset the Android Advertising ID.

Support email

If you email oojoo50@gmail.com, we hold that message. You can ask us to access or delete it by writing to the same address. We act without undue delay after we confirm the request, and within one month where the GDPR applies.

If the California privacy law applies to us, we still do not sell personal information for money. To refuse the use of identifiers for interest-based ads, use the controls in Section 8.

10. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

  • Support email: our legitimate interest in replying to a message you sent. Where the law sets a retention period for complaint or dispute records, that legal obligation applies to those emails.
  • Personalized advertising using device identifiers and device storage: your consent given in the UMP consent form. If you do not consent, Google serves limited ads.
  • Ad serving, fraud prevention related to ads, and approximate region (IP): our legitimate interest in funding the free game with ads. Google holds this information. We do not hold it per person.
  • iOS advertising identifier (IDFA): your consent when you allow tracking in the system prompt. If you do not allow tracking, we do not collect the IDFA.
  • Android advertising identifier outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland: AdMob may use it while the Advertising ID is turned on. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland, UMP consent applies.

We do not process personal information in order to provide the game as a contract. Progress stays on the device.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea, or the minimum age set by law in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The App does not ask for age and does not tag ad requests for child-directed treatment.

If a parent or guardian emails oojoo50@gmail.com, we delete any support email we hold about that child. Advertising records are held by Google; we cannot identify the child's device and delete those records.

12. Automatic Data Collection Technologies

The App does not use web cookies. It uses advertising identifiers and the AdMob SDK as described above.

13. Security

  • Communication with Google AdMob is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Game progress stays on the device and is not uploaded.
  • We do not run our own user personal-information database. The AdMob console is accessed with a Google account.
