Tumblix — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") publishes the mobile app Tumblix (Android package name and iOS bundle ID: com.cdplus.tumblix.app, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, who holds it, and the choices you have.

This document is not legal advice. It describes the App as implemented in code.

1. Information We Collect

You can play without creating an account or signing in with your name. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, postal address, precise GPS location, contacts, or photos as part of normal play.

Held only on your device (not collected by CDPLUS): game progress (stars, cleared levels, coins, endless best), settings (volume, haptics, language, accessibility), and daily-streak counters. These stay in on-device storage (SharedPreferences). We do not upload them to a CDPLUS server or any cloud backup. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data, or using Reset progress in Settings, removes them from that device. Progress cannot be restored on another device.

The App does not use Google AdMob, Firebase, Analytics, or Crashlytics. There is no account, no server, and no in-app purchase. There is no separate EEA/UK/Switzerland in-app consent dialog. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency or collect IDFA.

If you write to us: whatever you include in that email is held in the mailbox that receives it.

2. How We Use Information

Purpose What is used
Providing the game on your device Device-only progress (not collected by us)
Answering support email you send us Whatever you include in that email

We do not send marketing newsletters. We do not operate a purchase-verification server of our own. We do not run ads.

3. How Long Information Is Kept

Data Retention
Device-only game data Not held by us; removed when you uninstall, clear app data, or tap Reset progress
Support emails (if you write to us) Up to 1 year after resolution; up to 3 years if the message is a complaint or dispute record required by Korean law

4. Processors

We do not send play data to processors. Apple and Google operate their own app-store services independently of us.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share device identifiers with advertising partners. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. International Transfers

Game progress kept only on your device is not transferred by us. Support email may be stored in the mailbox that receives it, which may be outside Korea.

7. Deletion

  • Device data: uninstall the App, clear app data in system settings, or tap Reset progress in Settings. We do not keep a server or cloud copy.
  • Support email: you may ask us to delete messages we hold at the contact address below.

We do not have a process to locate and delete Google-held advertising or analytics records, because this App does not send those records.

8. Ads and Tracking Choices

The App does not show ads and does not request tracking permission. There is no in-app consent dialog for personalized ads.

9. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing for information we hold. If we hold none beyond optional support email, we will say so. GDPR may also give you portability and objection rights.

  • Device data: delete via uninstall, clear app data, or Reset progress. We do not keep a copy.
  • Support email: contact the address below. Where GDPR applies we aim to reply within one month.
  • A representative may need to show authority.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not keep a user-level sale/share roster. Payment records, if any, are held by Apple or Google under their procedures.

10. Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Support email: legitimate interest in answering you; legal obligation only where a complaint record must be kept.
  • We do not rely on “contract performance” for processing personal data to run the game, because game progress on device is not collected by us as a personal profile.
  • We do not process advertising identifiers. We do not keep transaction records of our own.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not ask for age and do not mark requests as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. A parent may ask us to delete support email we hold.

12. Automatic Collection

The App does not use web cookies. It does not send device identifiers to advertising or analytics SDKs.

13. Security

  • Any support email in transit uses TLS on the mail path.
  • Game progress stays on the device. There is no cloud backup.
  • We do not store card numbers.
  • We do not operate a user personal-data database of our own.
