Lottora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-08

This policy applies to Lottora (Android package com.cdplus.lottora.app, iOS bundle ID com.cdplus.lottora.app), a local lottery-number draw app published by CdPlus ("we"). It describes what the current release actually does.

1. Information we collect

  • Information we collect ourselves: none. The app has no account, no login, and no company server. We do not keep a personal-information file that can be looked up by name or email address. The only personal information we may hold is an email you choose to send us.
  • Data stored only on your device: draw history, settings, language choice, custom lottery definitions, and generated numbers are stored locally (an on-device Hive database and local files). They are never uploaded to us. They disappear from that device when you uninstall the app or clear its data, and they cannot be restored on another device.
  • Saved ticket images: when you choose to save a ticket, the operating system's photo-library permission is used to write the image to your photo library. We do not upload or keep that image. When you choose to share a ticket, the image is handed to the system share sheet and goes only to the app or person you pick.
  • Font downloads: for some languages the app downloads font files from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) the first time they are needed. This request reveals your IP address to Google; no other data is sent.
  • Information processed by Google: the app shows interstitial ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). When ads are requested, Google may process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID or iOS IDFV/IDFA where the system allows it), IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. The app also includes Firebase Core to connect to its Firebase project. It does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, authentication, Firestore, or Cloud Storage, and the app does not log custom events.

2. Purpose of use

  • Data on your device: to provide the app's features on that device. We collect nothing for this purpose.
  • Google Mobile Ads: to serve and measure ads, limit ad frequency, and detect invalid traffic, which keeps the app free.
  • Contact email: to reply to your inquiry.

3. Retention period

  • On your device: until you delete the app or clear its data.
  • With us: we have no server copy of app or advertising data. An inquiry email is kept for one year after it has been handled; an email that records a complaint or dispute may be kept for three years as required by applicable law.
  • With Google: advertising records are kept by Google under Google's own retention policies.

4. Processors

  • Google LLC (Google Mobile Ads, Firebase, Google Fonts): ad serving and measurement, app configuration for the Firebase project, and font file delivery.

5. Provision to third parties

We do not sell or provide your personal information to third parties. Ad requests are sent directly from the app to Google. Personalized ads may use your advertising identifier only when, on iOS, tracking is allowed in system settings, or on Android, the advertising ID is enabled; in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, only after you consent in Google's consent form (UMP).

6. International transfer

Ad requests and font downloads may be processed by Google on servers outside your country, including the United States, when the app requests them over the network. Data on your device and saved ticket images are not transferred abroad. An inquiry email is stored in our email provider's mailbox, which may be outside your country.

7. Destruction

We delete inquiry emails after the retention period above. Advertising records are not on our servers and are deleted by Google according to Google's retention schedule. Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Advertising and tracking choices

  • Consent form: where required (for example in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown when the app starts. Ads are requested only after the consent status allows it.
  • Android: you can delete or reset the advertising ID in the device's Google or privacy settings.
  • iOS: you can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Turning off advertising identifiers limits personalized ads, but non-personalized ads may still be shown.

9. Your rights

  • You may request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information. Where the GDPR applies, you may also request restriction of processing, object to processing, and request a copy. If we hold no information about you, we will tell you so and close the request. Exercising your rights does not limit your use of the app.
  • Data on your device: delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system settings. We have no copy.
  • Advertising records: limit them through device settings (iOS tracking setting, Android advertising ID). We have no procedure to locate and delete an individual's records held by Google, because we cannot identify those records from an email address, device model, app version, or time of use.
  • Inquiry emails: you may ask to see or delete them at the contact address below. Where the GDPR applies, we reply within one month. If a representative makes a request, we may ask for proof of authorization.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Where California law applies to us, opting out of personalized ads is done through the device settings above. We keep no per-user list of sold or shared personal information.

10. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Inquiry emails: our legitimate interest in replying; complaint or dispute emails kept for a period set by law: legal obligation.
  • Use of device identifiers and information stored on the device for personalized ads: your consent given in the UMP consent form. If you do not consent, Google sends only limited ads.
  • Ad measurement, invalid-traffic prevention, and approximate location from IP: our legitimate interest in operating a free app. Google holds these records; we do not hold them per user.
  • Android advertising ID: in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland it follows your UMP consent; elsewhere AdMob uses it while the advertising ID is enabled, and you can delete or reset it in device settings.

11. Children

Lottora is not directed primarily at children. The app does not ask for age and does not mark ad requests as child-directed, so it does not distinguish children from other users. A parent or guardian may ask us to delete an inquiry email sent by their child; that is the only personal information we could hold.

12. Automatic collection

The app does not use cookies. Google Mobile Ads automatically collects the device and ad information described in section 1 when ads are requested. Firebase Core is included only to connect to the Firebase project; no analytics events are collected. Font downloads from Google Fonts send only the network request itself.

13. Security

Network communication with Google services uses TLS encryption. App data stays on your device. We do not store card numbers or payment information, and we have no user personal-information database or company server. The Firebase and AdMob consoles are accessed with Google accounts.

15. Changes
