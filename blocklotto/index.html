Block Lotto Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Block Lotto (the "app") is a mobile game provided by CDPlus (the "developer"). The Android package name and the iOS bundle identifier are com.cdplus.blocklotto.app.

This privacy policy explains what information the app processes, why, how long it is kept, and who receives it. Privacy questions and requests go to privacy@cdplus.app.

Not an official lottery

Block Lotto is an offline-first breakout-style game that generates lottery numbers for entertainment.

It is not an official lottery. The app does not sell tickets. It does not take bets. It does not pay prizes. Numbers, bonus numbers, and scores produced in the app are results of gameplay. They are not connected to any real draw, winning ticket, or payout.

Information we do not collect

The app does not create an account. We do not collect:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Card number, bank account, or billing address
  • Address book or contacts
  • Camera, microphone, or photo library contents
  • Health information
  • Precise location (GPS)

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose personal information to any third party other than Google AdMob and Google Firebase, as described below. Only the in-app purchase payment is processed by Apple or Google on the store account.

Information stored on the device

The following information is stored only in app storage on the device (SharedPreferences):

  • Generated lottery tickets: 6 numbers per set, an optional bonus number, score, chapter title, level title, and date
  • Level progress and best scores
  • Set settings: how many sets a round creates, and whether the 100-set unlock is owned
  • Settings: language, music on or off, and sound effects on or off
  • A random anonymous installation id: a UUID created on the device. It is not linked to a name or an account.

We process this information to continue the game, keep generated numbers on the device, and remember language and sound settings. There is no server account, so this storage has no password.

In-app purchase

Choosing up to 100 sets is a non-consumable in-app product (blocklotto_sets_100). Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment. This app does not receive a card number, bank account, or billing address. After purchase, the device stores only whether the unlock is owned. Restore uses the store account.

Sharing

When the user taps Share, the app opens the operating system share sheet. The sheet contains text and, when used, an image of the ticket.

The app does not upload that text or image to the developer. The destination app is chosen by the user. After the user sends the content, that app and its operator handle it under their own policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob interstitial ads and rewarded ads. An interstitial is requested when a round ends and the result screen opens. A rewarded ad is requested when the user chooses to watch an ad for a bonus number or an extra ball.

When an ad is requested, Google may process the following under Google's policies:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Approximate location

These items are separate from what the app stores in SharedPreferences. The app does not collect precise location. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies.

On Android, the app uses the AD_ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) so the advertising ID can be used. On iOS, the app may show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. The user's choice is reflected in the device's tracking settings.

A rewarded ad is not requested unless the user chooses to watch one. An interstitial is requested when a round ends. The advertising ID can be reset or limited in the device settings.

Firebase

The app uses the Firebase project blocklotto-5bee0. There is no account login. The scope is limited to the following:

  • Firebase Analytics: app open, level clear, and score. No name and no email.
  • Cloud Firestore: a copy of the lottery ticket and the anonymous installation id, so history can be restored later. The ticket copy has the same contents as the ticket stored on the device: 6 numbers, an optional bonus number, score, chapter title, level title, and date.
  • Crash data is not collected. The app does not use Crashlytics.

Firebase processing is described in Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.

Why we process information

  • To keep game progress, scores, and generated numbers on the device
  • To keep language and sound settings
  • To keep an anonymous installation id that distinguishes records on the device
  • To show an interstitial when a round ends, and a rewarded ad when the user chooses to watch one
  • To measure app open, level clear, and score with Firebase Analytics, and to restore ticket history with Cloud Firestore

We do not use the information for other purposes unless the law requires it.

How long we keep information

  • Information on the device: It stays until the user clears it in the app settings or uninstalls the app. Uninstalling the app removes that app's storage through the operating system.
  • Cloud Firestore: Records are kept until the user emails a deletion request to privacy@cdplus.app and that request is confirmed. We then delete the ticket copy and installation id.
  • Ads and Analytics: Retention of data held by Google follows Google's policies.

Third parties

We do not sell personal information.

The only external recipients are:

Google AdMob

  • Recipient: Google
  • Items: advertising ID, IP address, device information, and approximate location
  • When: when a round ends and the result screen opens, and when the user chooses to watch an ad for a bonus number or an extra ball
  • Purpose: to provide and measure interstitial and rewarded ads
  • Retention: as set out in Google's policies

Google Firebase

  • Recipient: Google
  • Firebase Analytics: app open, level clear, and score. No name and no email.
  • Cloud Firestore: a copy of the lottery ticket and the anonymous installation id
  • Purpose: usage statistics and restoration of history
  • Retention: Analytics follows Google's policies. Firestore records are kept until the user's deletion request is confirmed.

If the user picks another app in the operating system share sheet, that is the user's own share. It is not a disclosure by the developer to that app.

Service providers

Google Firebase stores ticket history and processes app open, level clear, and score statistics for the developer. The services are Cloud Firestore and Firebase Analytics. The provider is Google. Name and email are not sent. Crash reporting (Crashlytics) is not part of this arrangement.

Advertising ID, IP address, device information, and approximate location that accompany an ad request are processed by Google under Google's advertising policies.

International transfers

Google AdMob and Google Firebase may process information on servers outside Korea.

  • Recipient: Google
  • Countries: countries where Google operates servers. Those servers may be outside Korea.
  • Items and purposes: the same scope as in the third-party section.
  • Timing and method: when a round ends and when the user chooses to watch an ad, and when the app is used and tickets are stored. Transmission is over the network.
  • Retention: as stated in the retention section.
  • Safeguards: Google applies the standard safeguards described in its privacy policy and data processing terms, including standard contractual clauses.

To object to an international transfer, email privacy@cdplus.app. Users in Korea may also request suspension of processing at the same address, as described under Your rights. We review the request and reply under applicable law.

Deletion

Information on the device is removed when the user clears it in the app settings or uninstalls the app. Ticket copies and the anonymous installation id in Firestore are deleted after a deletion request is confirmed. Electronic files are deleted in a way that they are not restored.

Your rights

Email privacy@cdplus.app to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.

Under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, a user may request access, correction, deletion, and suspension of processing. We review the request within the period required by law and explain the result. If the law allows us to refuse, we explain the reason.

There is no account password. To find the right record, we may ask for information that identifies it, such as the anonymous installation id.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe information of a child under 14 has been collected, email privacy@cdplus.app. We will delete it after we confirm the request.

Security

Personal information is kept in app storage on the device. There is no password because there is no account. The operating system keeps that storage separate from other apps. Information leaves the device only for the Google services described in this policy, and only when those services are actually in use. Those services use their own secure connections.
