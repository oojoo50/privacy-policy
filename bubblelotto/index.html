Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Applicable Application: BubbleLotto (Android Package: com.cdplus.bubblelotto.app, iOS Bundle ID: com.cdplus.bubblelotto.app)

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it in compliance with applicable data protection laws including GDPR and CCPA. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data handling in the BubbleLotto mobile application ("the App").

1. Information Collection and Storage

The App is a self-contained, offline puzzle and lottery number generation game that operates locally on your device.

  • Local Device Storage:
  • Gameplay progress, high scores, generated number archives, and user settings.
  • Stored locally using SQLite (sqflite) and device preferences (shared_preferences).
  • No Server-Side Collection:
  • We do not operate external member databases, account systems, or remote servers.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on developer servers.
  • The App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs (such as Google Mobile Ads or Firebase Analytics).
  • Information Not Collected:
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
  • Device location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID).

2. Purpose of Use

All data handled by the App is used exclusively on your local device to:

  • Save and restore puzzle sessions and high scores.
  • Store customized lottery number sets and favorite rules.
  • Retain user preferences such as sound effects, haptic feedback, reduced motion, and language settings.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data resides strictly on your local device.
  • Data is retained until you uninstall the App or clear application data via device system settings. Deletion permanently removes all stored data.

4. Entrustment of Data Processing

We do not entrust personal data processing to any third-party processors.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. When you explicitly use the "Share" or "Export" functions within the App, data is shared only with the external destination you select (such as a messaging app or email client).

6. Cross-Border Data Transfers

We do not collect or transfer personal data across borders.

7. Data Destruction Procedures

  • Procedure: Stored game data is destroyed immediately when the App is uninstalled or when you clear data via device settings.
  • Method: Local database and preference files are permanently deleted by the operating system.

8. Advertising and Tracking (No Ads)

  • The App contains no advertisements and does not display tailored or generic advertisements.
  • The App does not request or use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework or Android Advertising ID.

9. User Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You have full control over your data directly on your device.
  • You can reset preferences or delete saved number archives within the App, or uninstall the App to erase all records.
  • Because no personal data is transmitted to our servers, there are no remote records to delete. For general inquiries, you can reach us at oojoo50@gmail.com.

10. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • We do not process personal data on external servers. Local storage on your device is conducted solely to perform the core gameplay features requested by the user (performance of a contract).

11. Children's Privacy

  • The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age thresholds).

12. Automated Collection and Cookies

  • The App does not use browser cookies, tracking beacons, or online behavioral analytics.

13. Security Safeguards

  • No personal data is transmitted over public networks.
  • All local data is isolated within the operating system's standard application sandbox.
