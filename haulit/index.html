HaulIt Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

HaulIt is a local construction hauling game for Android and iOS. The app identifier is com.cdplus.haulit.app.

Information collected

HaulIt does not require an account and does not send game progress, settings, or device files to a company server. Progress, settings, and local records are stored on the device by the platform preferences service.

HaulIt includes Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads and transition interstitials, and the platform in-app purchase services for the non-consumable haulit_remove_ads product. Google and the platform stores may process device, advertising, purchase, and diagnostic information under their own policies. HaulIt does not receive payment card details or operate its own analytics, account, location, contacts, or camera service.

How local data is used

Local data is used only to restore game progress, settings, language choice, and best results on the same device. It is not available to us and cannot be transferred to another device by HaulIt. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes this local data.

Third parties and international transfers

HaulIt does not send game progress or settings to a company server. Ad requests are made through Google Mobile Ads, and purchase transactions are handled by Google Play or the App Store. The app has no cloud save service; those providers may process technical information under their own policies when ads are requested or purchases are restored.

Deletion and user rights

You can delete local HaulIt data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in device settings. Because HaulIt does not maintain an account or a server-side personal-information record, we cannot identify or delete a separate copy for an individual user. You can send requests to exercise your rights to oojoo50@gmail.com; because we hold no personal-information record about you, we will tell you so and close the request. If a request is made through an agent, we may ask for proof of authorisation.

Children

HaulIt is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Security

Game data and the local remove-ads entitlement remain in device storage managed by the operating system. HaulIt does not store payment card details or operate a personal-information database. Keep your device and operating system protected with the security controls provided by the platform.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your privacy, email oojoo50@gmail.com. Messages you send are kept only to answer you.
