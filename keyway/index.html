Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Applicable Application: Keyway (Android Package: com.cdplus.keyway.app, iOS Bundle ID: com.cdplus.keyway.app)

Developer: CDPLUS (oojoo50@gmail.com)

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the Keyway mobile application ("the App").

1. Information Collected and Stored

The App operates primarily offline on your device, with third-party advertising, analytics, and in-app purchase services.

1-1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress: Cleared stage levels, star ratings, best movement counts, daily puzzle completion records, and user preferences (sound mute, vibration, selected language).
  • Storage Location: Stored strictly on your local device via local storage (shared_preferences).
  • No Developer Server Storage: We do not operate external membership databases, user accounts, or cloud save servers. Gameplay progress is never uploaded to our servers.

1-2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

When you use the App, Google AdMob and Firebase may automatically collect certain technical identifiers and usage data to provide ads, analytics, and crash diagnostics:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA when tracking permission is granted), vendor ID (IDFV), and Firebase instance identifiers.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic region (country or city level) estimated from your IP address by Google AdMob for ad serving. Precise GPS location is never requested or collected.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Screen views, gameplay session duration, level completions, and ad interactions (impressions, clicks).
  • Diagnostics: Application crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics) and performance diagnostics (launch latency, rendering performance).
  • In-App Purchases: Transaction validation is processed directly through Apple App Store or Google Play Store billing systems. We do not receive, collect, or store payment card numbers, bank accounts, or financial credentials.

2. Purposes of Processing

Data handled through third-party SDKs is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Game Operation: Saving and restoring puzzle progress and settings on your device (no personal information processed by the developer).
  • Advertising: Displaying non-personalized or personalized banner/interstitial/rewarded ads via Google AdMob, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraudulent ad traffic.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Analyzing aggregated gameplay trends and crash statistics via Firebase to improve game balance and fix bugs.
  • Purchase Processing: Fulfilling digital item entitlement (such as ad removal) via Google Play and Apple App Store in-app purchase systems.

3. Data Retention Period

  • Local Device Data: Retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear application data in device settings.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics Data: Stored by Google for up to 14 months from collection according to Google's standard retention policies.
  • Inquiry Emails: If you send an inquiry to our support email, the correspondence is retained for 1 year for customer service resolution, or 3 years if related to consumer dispute settlement as required by law.
  • Developer Retention: We do not maintain a separate database of users; therefore, no personal user records are retained on developer servers.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may be shared with third parties only in the following cases:

  • Advertising Partners: When you consent to tracking on iOS or use standard Android advertising IDs, advertising identifiers and interaction data may be shared through Google AdMob with certified ad networks for ad delivery.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable laws, regulations, or legitimate legal warrants.

6. International Transfers

Our third-party providers (Google LLC and Apple Inc.) operate global infrastructure. Data processed by Firebase and AdMob is transferred over encrypted networks (HTTPS/TLS) to data centers located in the United States and other global regions. These transfers are necessary for ad delivery, analytics, and billing verification.

7. Data Destruction Procedures

  • Local Data: Game data is permanently erased immediately when the App is uninstalled or cleared via your device's system settings.
  • Third-Party Data: Data processed by Google AdMob and Firebase is automatically purged according to Google's retention schedules.
  • Inquiry Emails: Permanently deleted from our support email inbox upon expiration of the retention period.

8. Advertising and Tracking Choices

The App is free-to-play and supported by advertisements (interstitial and rewarded interstitial ads).

  • Apple iOS (App Tracking Transparency): The App requests permission before accessing the IDFA. If you decline, non-targeted ads are displayed. You can modify this setting anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android (Advertising ID): You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Settings > Google > Ads. Once deleted, ads are served without advertising identifiers.
  • Ad-Free Option: You may permanently remove advertisements via the in-app purchase item "Remove Ads".

9. User Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You have the right to request access to, correction, or deletion of personal data held by us, or to request suspension of processing.
  • Because we do not store personal profiles, accounts, or identifiers on developer servers, we cannot identify individual users from email, device model, or gameplay time to look up or delete analytics/ad logs stored on Google's systems.
  • To delete all game progress, uninstall the App or clear data in your device settings.
  • For inquiries or deletion requests regarding customer support emails, contact us at oojoo50@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days (or without undue delay).

10. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, processing is conducted on the following bases:

  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Operating the free game through ad support, crash diagnostics, fraud prevention, and responding to user support emails.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Personalized advertising on iOS where tracking authorization is granted via ATT.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Handling and retaining customer dispute records where required by applicable laws.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 14 in the Republic of Korea). We do not implement age screening or child-directed ad profiling. If a parent or guardian discovers that inquiry correspondence containing a child's information was sent to us, please contact oojoo50@gmail.com for immediate deletion.

12. Automated Collection Technologies

The App does not use browser cookies or tracking beacons. Technical identifiers and SDK analytics events are collected solely via the standard Firebase and AdMob SDKs described above.

13. Security Safeguards

  • All network communications between the App and third-party SDK endpoints are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Game progress is stored only in the sandbox storage of your local device and is never uploaded to an external database.
  • Payment credentials (card numbers, passwords) are managed entirely by Apple and Google and never touch our servers.
