Orbital Strike — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

CDPLUS ("we", "us", or "our") publishes the mobile game Orbital Strike (Android package name and iOS bundle ID: com.cdplus.orbitalstrike.app, the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, how it is used, stored and shared, and the choices and rights you have.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the developer policies of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

You can start playing without creating an account or signing in. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, postal address, precise GPS location, contacts or photos. Game progress (stage records, gold, equipment, survival records and settings) stays on your device. We do not send it to our servers or store it in the cloud.

We do not keep a file of personal information that we can look up by name or email. The only information we hold ourselves is an email you choose to send us. Everything else below is sent to Google's analytics and advertising services. It is not stored on our servers.

Category Data How it is collected Required?
Device identifiers Firebase installation ID, app instance ID, App Set ID (Android) Collected automatically by Firebase Analytics While you use the App
Advertising identifiers Android Advertising ID (AAID). iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the system prompt Collected automatically by the ads SDK Optional (you can turn this off in device settings and in the consent dialog)

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with third parties except as follows:

  • If you allow tracking on iOS, or if the Android Advertising ID is turned on, and, in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, if you agree in the consent dialog, your advertising identifier and usage data may be passed to Google AdMob and the advertising partners that serve ads through it, for interest-based advertising. Each of them handles that data under its own privacy policy. Where the CCPA applies to us, this can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can limit it as described in Section 8. We do not keep a per-person sale or share list.
  • When required by law, or when requested by a public authority through a lawful process.

7. How Data Is Deleted

  • The information we hold is support email. When its retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled, we delete it without delay. Emails we must keep by law are deleted when that period ends.
  • Emails are deleted so they cannot be recovered. Analytics, crash and advertising records are not on our servers. Google deletes them on its own schedule.

8. Ads, Tracking and Your Choices

The App is free to play and supported by interstitial ads shown after a match result (not during active play) and by rewarded ads you choose to watch. You may remove interstitial ads through an optional in-app purchase.

Consent dialog (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland)

  • On first launch, the App shows Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) where the law requires it. You can agree to or refuse interest-based ads.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy options.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

  • Before accessing your IDFA, the App asks for your permission through the system prompt. If you choose not to allow tracking, we do not collect the IDFA and ads are shown without tracking.
  • You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android Advertising ID

  • On your device, go to Settings > Google > Ads to delete or reset your Advertising ID (menu names may vary by device and OS version). Once deleted, it can no longer be used for personalized ads.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see ads (unless you purchased remove-ads), but they will not be based on your interests.

9. Your Rights and How to Use Them

We do not create user accounts. We have no file of personal information we can look up by name or email. Game progress and the remove-ads flag stay on your device. Usage, crash and advertising data are held by Google (Firebase and AdMob). An email that gives only your address, device type, app version or when you played is not enough for us to find, correct or delete that Google record.

Under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act you may ask to access, correct, delete or suspend processing of personal information we hold. If the GDPR applies to you, you may also ask us to restrict or stop processing and to receive a copy. If we do not hold the information, we will tell you that and close the request. We do not restrict your use of the App because you made a request.

On the device

Uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings. Game progress and the remove-ads flag on that device are removed. We have no copy. Deleted progress cannot be restored and cannot be loaded on another device. If you bought remove-ads, the store still has the payment record. Restoring purchases in the App applies remove-ads on that device again.

Limiting analytics and ads

Use the consent dialog's privacy options, turn off iOS tracking, or delete or reset the Android Advertising ID, as described in Section 8. That is the control available to you. We do not run a process that takes a deletion email and locates your analytics or advertising record at Google.

Support email

If you email oojoo50@gmail.com, we hold that message. You can ask us to access or delete it by writing to the same address. We act without undue delay after we confirm the request, and within one month where the GDPR applies. If an agent makes the request, we may ask for proof of authorization.

Access to and deletion of payment records follow Apple's or Google's process. If the GDPR applies to you, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

If the California privacy law applies to us, we still do not sell personal information for money. To refuse the use of identifiers for interest-based ads, use the settings in Section 8. We do not keep a per-person sale or share list.

10. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

  • Support email: our legitimate interest in replying to a message you sent. Where the law sets a retention period for complaint or dispute records, that legal obligation applies to those emails.
  • Use of device identifiers and information stored on the device for interest-based ads: your consent, given in the consent dialog. If you do not consent, Google serves only limited ads.
  • Usage data, crash reports, invalid-click prevention and approximate location (IP address): our legitimate interest in running a free game and understanding how it is played. Google holds this information. We do not hold it per person.
  • iOS advertising identifier (IDFA): your consent, given when you allow tracking in the system prompt. If you do not allow tracking, we do not collect the IDFA.
  • Android advertising identifier: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it follows your choice in the consent dialog. Elsewhere, AdMob uses it while the Advertising ID is turned on in device settings, and you can delete or reset it as described in Section 8.

We do not process personal information in order to provide the game or to complete an in-app purchase. Progress and the remove-ads flag stay on the device, and payment records are kept by Apple and Google. Keeping transaction records is not a legal basis for processing by us.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea, or the minimum age set by law in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

The App does not ask for age and does not tell children apart from other users. Ad requests are not tagged for child-directed treatment.

If a parent or guardian emails oojoo50@gmail.com to say a child's information was processed, we delete any support email we hold about that child. Analytics and advertising records are held by Google. We cannot identify the child's device and delete those records.

12. Automatic Data Collection Technologies

The App does not use web cookies. It does use the device and advertising identifiers, analytics and crash reporting SDKs described above. You can opt out of ad tracking as described in Section 8. No sensitive information is included in the usage data we collect.

13. Security

  • All communication is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Game progress and the remove-ads flag stay on the device and are not uploaded.
  • Payment details are handled by Apple or Google. We do not store card numbers or similar payment credentials.
  • We do not run our own server or an administrator database of users' personal information. The Firebase and AdMob consoles are accessed with a Google account.
