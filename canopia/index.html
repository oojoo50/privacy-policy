Canopia Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Canopia mobile game ("Canopia", "the app") handles information. Canopia is an offline single-player game. It has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

  • App name: Canopia
  • Android package name: com.cdplus.canopia.app
  • iOS bundle identifier: com.cdplus.canopia.app

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Canopia does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

The app stores the following game data only on your device:

  • Game progress, unlocked stages, stars, scores and discoveries
  • In-game currency, seeds, upgrades and unlocked content
  • Settings such as language, sound levels, text size, colour-blind mode and graphics quality
  • Tutorial and story flags, and the dates used for daily streaks and daily bonuses

This data is stored with the device's local storage (SharedPreferences) inside the app's own storage area. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

2. How We Use Information

We do not collect or use personal information. The game data stored on your device is used only to run the game: to keep your progress, restore your settings and show your records when you open the app.

3. Retention Period

We hold no personal information, so there is nothing for us to retain. Game data remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in your device settings removes it from that device.

4. Service Providers

Canopia uses no third-party service providers that receive your data. There is no analytics service, advertising network, crash reporting service, cloud save or backend database connected to the app.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, share or disclose personal information, because we do not collect it. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK that sends data to a third party.

6. International Transfers

We do not transfer personal information across borders. Game data stays on your device and is not sent to any country.

7. Deletion

We hold no personal information and therefore have nothing to delete on our side. To remove game data, uninstall Canopia or clear its app data in your device settings. The data is then removed from that device. Progress is not backed up to a server, so it cannot be restored on another device.

8. Advertising and Tracking Choices

Canopia shows no advertising and contains no advertising identifier, tracking technology or consent management tool. There is nothing to opt out of. The app does not request tracking permission on iOS and does not read the Android advertising ID.

9. Your Rights

You may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of the processing of your personal information, and to object to processing or receive a copy of your data. Because we hold no personal information about you, we have nothing to disclose or correct. If you contact us, we will confirm that we hold no such data and close the request. Exercising these rights does not limit your use of the app.

You can delete all game data yourself by uninstalling the app or clearing its app data in your device settings. We keep no copy.

If the GDPR applies, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Legal Basis under the GDPR

We do not process personal information for the operation of Canopia, so no legal basis for such processing applies. If you send us an inquiry by email, we process your email address and message to answer you on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to you. We do not use inquiry emails for marketing.

11. Children

Canopia is a general-audience game and is not directed primarily at children. We do not ask for age, do not verify age and do not show child-directed advertising. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children.

12. Automatic Collection

Canopia does not automatically collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage records, crash logs or approximate location. The app does not request the Android internet permission, and it does not communicate with a server.

13. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, protected by the operating system's app sandbox. We hold no user database and operate no server. Because no data is transmitted, no network encryption applies to game data. Canopia does not process payment card numbers or payment information.

15. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. If a future version of the app adds data collection, we will update this policy before that version is released.
