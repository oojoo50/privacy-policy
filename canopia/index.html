Canopia Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Canopia mobile game ("Canopia", "the app") handles information. Canopia is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The free app shows ads served by Google AdMob.

  • App name: Canopia
  • Android package name: com.cdplus.canopia.app
  • iOS bundle identifier: com.cdplus.canopia.app

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. Canopia does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

The app stores the following game data only on your device:

  • Game progress, unlocked stages, stars, scores and discoveries
  • In-game currency, seeds, upgrades and unlocked content
  • Settings such as language, sound levels, text size, colour-blind mode and graphics quality
  • Tutorial and story flags, and the dates used for daily streaks and daily bonuses

This data is stored with the device's local storage (SharedPreferences) inside the app's own storage area. It is not sent to us.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) running in the app may collect and process, on Google's side, device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), the IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language) and ad interaction records. We do not receive this data and do not store it on any server of ours.

2. How We Use Information

We do not collect or use personal information ourselves. The game data stored on your device is used only to run the game: to keep your progress, restore your settings and show your records when you open the app.

Ads are shown to support the free app. Google uses the information described in Section 1 to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

3. Retention Period

We hold no personal information, so there is nothing for us to retain. Game data remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in your device settings removes it from that device.

Information processed by Google AdMob is held by Google under Google's own retention practices and is not stored by us.

4. Service Providers

Canopia uses one third-party service provider that receives data about your device: Google AdMob (Google LLC), for advertising. There is no analytics service, crash reporting service, cloud save or backend database connected to the app.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell personal information and we do not receive any personal information from the app. The Google Mobile Ads SDK sends the information in Section 1 directly from your device to Google. Depending on your consent and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may use it to show personalised ads.

6. International Transfers

We do not transfer personal information across borders. Game data stays on your device. Ad requests go directly from your device to Google, which may process data in countries outside your own. Google handles that processing under its own privacy terms.

7. Deletion

We hold no personal information and therefore have nothing to delete on our side. To remove game data, uninstall Canopia or clear its app data in your device settings. The data is then removed from that device. Progress is not backed up to a server, so it cannot be restored on another device. Advertising records held by Google are deleted according to Google's retention schedule and your Google and device settings.

8. Advertising and Tracking Choices

Canopia shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob.

  • Consent (UMP): When the app starts, Canopia uses Google's User Messaging Platform. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form may appear. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > "Privacy options", which is shown when it applies to you.
  • iOS: The consent flow may ask for tracking permission through Apple's system prompt. You can change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not available to the app.
  • Android: You can delete or reset the advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Turning these off does not remove ads. They will still appear, but may be less relevant.
  • We do not direct ads at children and have not enabled child-directed treatment.

9. Your Rights

You may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of the processing of your personal information, and to object to processing or receive a copy of your data. We hold no personal information about you, so we have nothing to disclose or correct. If you contact us, we will confirm that we hold no such data and close the request. Exercising these rights does not limit your use of the app.

You can delete all game data yourself by uninstalling the app or clearing its app data in your device settings. We keep no copy. Advertising data is held by Google; you can limit its use through your device's tracking or advertising ID settings and through the "Privacy options" screen in the app, or manage your data through your Google account's ad settings.

We do not sell personal information for money. We keep no list of users whose data is sold or shared. To opt out of personalised ads, use your device settings described in Section 8.

If the GDPR applies, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Legal Basis under the GDPR

We do not process personal information ourselves for the operation of Canopia, so no legal basis for such processing applies on our side. If you send us an inquiry by email, we process your email address and message to answer you on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to you. We do not use inquiry emails for marketing.

For advertising in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the use of device identifiers and device storage for personalised ads is based on your consent given in the consent form. If you do not consent, Google serves only limited, non-personalised ads. Measurement of ads, fraud prevention and approximate location from the IP address are based on the legitimate interest of operating a free app and are carried out and held by Google. On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking. On Android outside those regions, AdMob uses the advertising ID while it is enabled on your device.

11. Children

Canopia is a general-audience game and is not directed primarily at children. We do not ask for age, do not verify age and do not show child-directed advertising. We do not collect personal information from anyone. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above. If a parent or guardian has a concern, they can contact us at the address below.

12. Automatic Collection

Canopia itself does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage records, crash logs or location. Because the app shows ads, it uses the internet permission and, on Android, the advertising ID permission. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically sends the information in Section 1 to Google to serve ads. IP addresses may be used by Google to estimate approximate location. We do not receive this data.

13. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, protected by the operating system's app sandbox. We hold no user database and operate no server. Ad requests are sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to Google over encrypted connections. Canopia does not process payment card numbers or payment information.

15. Changes to This Policy
